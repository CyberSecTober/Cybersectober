Learn. Build. Contribute. Secure.

October ends, but the knowledge stays. Share what you know about staying safe online, add it to a free open-source library, and earn verified badges. Your first contribution takes 2 to 5 minutes, and you don't need to code.

Three ways to take part

1. Share a post

2 minutes
  1. Post one security tip on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads or Medium with #CyberSecTOBER.
  2. Copy the link to your post.
  3. Click the button below, then Fork this repository if GitHub asks.
  4. Add one line at the bottom: - [What your post is about](your-post-link) (@your-github-username)
  5. Click Commit changes…, then Propose changes, then Create pull request twice.
Add my post

Earns awareness badges: Awareness Advocate for your first post, Signal Booster for 5, Awareness Ambassador for posting every week of October.

2. Add a tip

5 minutes
  1. Click the button below, then Fork this repository if GitHub asks.
  2. Add one line at the bottom: - **Short tip title**, one sentence explaining it. (@your-github-username)
  3. Click Commit changes…, then Propose changes, then Create pull request twice.
Add my tip See existing tips

Earns 3 points and the First Contribution badge. Please make sure your tip isn't already on the list.

3. Take on a challenge

15 to 60 minutes
  1. Pick a challenge: a glossary entry, a quiz, a short scam story or a translation. Have more to share? Try a security tool, turn your blog post into a guide, or write a checklist, walkthrough or lab.
  2. Open-to-all challenges need no claiming. For the others, comment "I'll take this" first.
  3. Follow the steps in the challenge and open a pull request.
Open-to-all challenges All beginner challenges Intermediate and advanced

Earns 3 to 20 points per contribution, plus tier and specialty badges.

What happens next

  1. Automatic checks look for duplicates and formatting problems, and tell you if anything needs fixing.
  2. A mentor reviews your contribution, usually within 48 hours.
  3. When it's merged, you get an email with your badge, one-click Add to LinkedIn and Share on X buttons, and a public verification page.
  4. Your name appears on the leaderboard.

Quick answers

Do I need to know how to code?No. Tips, posts, glossary entries, quizzes, stories and translations only need plain writing. Can I do it on my phone?Yes. Everything above works in your phone's browser. Is it free?Yes, completely. Contributions opened by 31 October 2026 count. What can't I share?Anything harmful: malware, phishing kits, real passwords, personal data or attacks on real organisations. Everything here is for education and defence only.
