# CyberSecTOBER 2026

[![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober?style=social)](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober)

**Learn. Build. Contribute. Secure.**

A month-long open-source cybersecurity challenge for Cybersecurity Awareness Month. Share what you know about staying safe online, add it to a free library anyone can use, and earn verified badges. Your first contribution takes 2 to 5 minutes, and you don't need to code.

**[How to join](https://cybersectober.github.io/Cybersectober/join/)** · **[Leaderboard](https://cybersectober.github.io/Cybersectober/)** · **[Open-to-all challenges](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aopen-to-all)** · **[Discussions](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/discussions)**

New to GitHub? [Create a free account](https://github.com/signup), sign in, and **star this repo** with the Star button at the top right of this page.

## Three ways to take part

### 1. Share a post (2 minutes)

1. Post one security tip on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads or Medium with **#CyberSecTOBER**.
2. Copy the link to your post.
3. **[Open the posts file for editing](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/edit/main/awareness/posts.md)**, then click **Fork this repository** if GitHub asks.
4. Add one line at the bottom: `- [What your post is about](your-post-link) (@your-github-username)`
5. Click **Commit changes…**, then **Propose changes**, then **Create pull request** twice.

Earns awareness badges: Awareness Advocate for your first post, Signal Booster for 5, Awareness Ambassador for posting every week of October.

### 2. Add a tip (5 minutes)

1. **[Open the tips file for editing](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/edit/main/awareness/tips.md)**, then click **Fork this repository** if GitHub asks.
2. Add one line at the bottom: `- **Short tip title**, one sentence explaining it. (@your-github-username)`
3. Click **Commit changes…**, then **Propose changes**, then **Create pull request** twice.

Earns 3 points and the First Contribution badge. Please check your tip isn't already in the [list of tips](awareness/tips.md).

### 3. Take on a challenge (15 to 60 minutes)

- **[Open-to-all challenges](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aopen-to-all)** need no claiming: a glossary entry, a quiz, a short scam story, a translation and more.
- **[Other beginner challenges](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)** are claimed first: comment **"I'll take this"** on the issue.
- Follow the steps in the challenge, then open a pull request. Need more detail? See the [full contributor guide](docs/guide.md#level-3-build-something-bigger-1560-minutes).

Earns 3 to 20 points per contribution, plus tier and specialty badges.

## What happens next

1. Automatic checks look for duplicates and formatting problems, and tell you if anything needs fixing.
2. A mentor reviews your contribution, usually within 48 hours.
3. When it's merged, you get an email with your badge, one-click **Add to LinkedIn** and **Share on X** buttons, and a public verification page.
4. Your name appears on the [leaderboard](https://cybersectober.github.io/Cybersectober/).

## Rules

Everything here is for **education and defence**. Never submit malware, phishing kits or copies of real login pages, attacks on real organisations, real passwords or API keys, personal data, or unpatched vulnerabilities in real products. Examples must use fictional companies and `example.com`. Full rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
