# Contributing to CyberSecTOBER

First time contributing to open source? Perfect, this project is built for you. 💙

⭐ **Before you start, star the repo:** click the **☆ Star** button at the top right of the [repo page](https://github.com/CyberSecTober/Cybersectober), next to **Fork** (on a phone, it's just under the repo name).

## The three ways to contribute

**1. Share a post (2 min).** Share a security tip on social media with #CyberSecTOBER, then add the link to [`awareness/posts.md`](awareness/posts.md) in your browser, one post per pull request. Posts earn awareness badges, not points.

**2. Submit (5 min, no code).** Either add a tip in your browser (see below) or use the [**Submit a tip or resource**](../../issues/new/choose) form. A maintainer adds form submissions to the repo and credits you as a co-author.

**3. Build (15–60 min).** Pick an [open issue](../../issues), comment "I'll take this", and open a pull request.

## Adding a tip in your browser

1. Open `awareness/tips.md` and click the ✏️ pencil icon
2. Add one line at the bottom, in this format:
   `- **Short tip title**, one sentence explaining it. (Your Name or @github-handle)`
3. Click **Commit changes…** → **Propose changes** → **Create pull request**

## Building a bigger contribution

- Comment on the issue to claim it (held for 5 days).
- Copy a template from `templates/` into the right track folder.
- Use lowercase, hyphenated names, e.g. `awareness/phishing/fake-bank-sms/`.
- Bigger pieces (labs, tools, research): open an issue first so a mentor can help early.
- Full guides, labs and tools should start with this header (small tips don't need it):

```yaml
---
title:
author:
track: awareness | api-security | ai-security | community
difficulty: beginner | intermediate | advanced
language:
description:
---
```

## What makes a good contribution

- **Useful**: a real person would benefit from it
- **Accurate**: correct and current; cite sources for statistics
- **Original**: your own words, no copying from courses, blogs or other repos
- **Clear**: plain language for its audience

Using AI tools to help is fine, but you're responsible for checking accuracy.

Don't worry about small formatting mistakes, because reviewers will fix those for you.

## 🛡️ Safety rules (non-negotiable)

**Never submit:** malware or ransomware, credential stealers, working phishing kits or clones of real login pages, attacks on real organizations or systems, real credentials or API keys, personal data or real victim information, or unpatched vulnerabilities in real products (use responsible disclosure instead).

Phishing examples must use fictional companies and `example.com`. Labs must be deliberately vulnerable and run locally or in a sandbox.

## What happens after you submit

1. A mentor reviews your contribution, usually within 48 hours
2. It gets merged 🎉
3. You get your badge and points in a comment on your PR
4. Your name goes on the leaderboard

PRs opened by **October 31** count, even if they're merged in November.
