# Security Tips

Add one security tip per line, with your name. Keep it short and practical.

- **Never share your OTP**, not even with someone claiming to be your bank. (@cybersectober team)
- **Use a password manager** instead of reusing the same password everywhere. (@cybersectober team)
- **Enable two-factor authentication** on every account that supports it, starting with your email. (@cybersectober team)
- **Check the sender's real address**, not just the display name, before you trust an email. (@DamilolaAbiona)
- **Devices: Protecting Privacy & Security**  Avoid storing personal passwords, private documents, financial information, or other sensitive personal data on a work device unless company policy permits it. (@Agbafamo)
- **Verify AI-generated links**, check the destination before clicking or sharing sensitive information, as attackers can use AI to create convincing phishing pages. (@priyanshi17112007)
- **Check linked devices regularly**, linked devices shows every computer or browser logged into your account. If you see a session you don't recognize, log it out right away. (@nyimenkabenson)
